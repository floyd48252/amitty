Our mission

AmityPoint Institute is a global policy and peacebuilding think tank committed to addressing the root causes of conflict, injustice, and governance failure. Founded on the belief that peace and public policy must intersect, we provide evidence-based research, practical solutions, and strategic insights to governments, civil society, and international institutions.

Amity

Represents harmony, collaboration, and peaceful relations across communities and nations.

Point

Stands for clarity, direction, and practical focus in policy and peacebuilding efforts.

Institute

Embodies thought leadership, learning, and the development of impactful ideas.
