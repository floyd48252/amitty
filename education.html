What we offer

From interactive workshops to publications and mentoring, our education portfolio supports effective decision-making and inclusive governance.

AmityPoint Institute is a global policy and peacebuilding think tank committed to addressing the root causes of conflict, injustice, and governance failure. Founded on the belief that peace and public policy must intersect, we provide evidence-based research, practical solutions, and strategic insights to governments, civil society, and international institutions.

Workshops

Custom learning experiences designed for public sector teams, civil society organizations, and academic partners.

Publications

Briefs, reports, and learning resources that make complex policy ideas accessible.

Mentorship

Guided support for emerging leaders who are shaping governance and peacebuilding practice.
